Privacy

Privacy, in plain words.

No cookies, no analytics, no ads. This page lists what data moves when you use cronoslegends.com, and where it goes.

Who runs this site

cronoslegends.com is run by Crovia Oy (Finland), the team behind crovia.app. For anything about this notice or your data, email contact@cronoslegends.com.

What we don't collect

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or ads.
  • No accounts, sign-ups or newsletters.
  • No database of our own behind this site. The only thing stored for us is the optional game leaderboard described below.

Your wallet

Wallets connect only on the burn page. When you connect, your address stays in your browser: the page uses it there to find your eligible NFTs and to prepare the transaction you confirm in your wallet. We never store your address and never send it to a server of ours.

The burn page remembers which wallet app you picked, not your address, in your browser's local storage, so it can reconnect next time. Disconnecting clears it.

Anything you do on-chain, like a burn, is public on Cronos forever. That's how blockchains work, and nobody can delete it, including us.

Services your browser talks to

Your browser talks to these outside services directly. Like any server, each one sees your IP address and the request it answers. The blockchain data providers also see which wallet address your browser asks about.

Trinari Defense

If you save a score, the name you type and your results (kills, wave, survival time and date) are stored in Google Firebase and shown publicly on the game's leaderboard. Use a nickname, not your real name. If saving to Firebase fails, the game keeps the score in your browser's local storage instead. If you'd like a leaderboard entry removed, email us.

Discord, X and other sites

Our Discord server and X account run on those platforms, under their own privacy policies. The same goes for the other sites we link to, such as VVS Finance and the Cronos explorer. crovia.app is also run by Crovia Oy and has its own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: contact@cronoslegends.com. If this notice changes, the new version replaces this page.
