Who controls what

$CLG token
Nobody. Ownership was renounced in 2024, and the contract has no mint, tax, blacklist or pause. See the transaction
Legends Awaken I and II
The contracts' admin and minter role belongs to Crypto.com NFT's deployer (0x083B…d843), the platform they launched on. We don't hold it.
Burn contracts
Once launched: no owner, no admin and no withdraw function. Each holds its own $CLG reserve, which can only leave as burn payouts.
