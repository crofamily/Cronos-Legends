Origin story

The First Impact

Long before the Trinari awakened in the forests and winds, there was the First Impact. A celestial meteor streaked across the sky, crashing into the realm with earth-shaking force.

From this cosmic event, the very first Trinari emerged: the Elderborn. Each was born from a single element the meteor's magic touched. Stone and lava. Wood and moss. Shadow, and a little golden light.

These are not just Trinari. These are the ancestors. The first. The Elderborn.

Questions

Good to know

When does the mint open?

The timing lives on the crovia.app launch page, so it's always current. The Discord hears the news first.

How much does an Elderborn cost?

The public mint is 400 CRO per Elderborn, up to 10 per wallet, plus a little CRO for gas. The launch page is the source of truth for price and phases.

What does "playable" mean?

Every Elderborn will be playable in Cronos City, the upcoming 3D game at cronos.city, and every holder can download their Elderborn's 3D model.

Can I burn an Elderborn for $CLG?

Yes, once the mint has sold out: each Elderborn burn pays $CLG in the same transaction. The payout value is revealed after the sell-out. Before the sell-out there is no Elderborn burn. Burning is permanent. Read the burn rules.

How do I avoid fake mint sites?

Only mint at crovia.app/launch/trinari-elderborn, and check the address bar before you connect a wallet. Ignore mint links in DMs and replies. We never DM first and never ask for your recovery phrase. Every official link is on one page.
