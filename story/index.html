Episode 1 · Before the awakening

The Fall of the Old World

Long ago, the world thrived under the guidance of the Cronos Guardians — noble protectors who upheld order and peace. At the heart of their realm stood a powerful Totem, pulsing with ancient energy — a symbol of balance and strength.

But from the shadows, the Chaos Horde emerged, carrying a Totem of their own that burned with a darker fire. Twisted by fury and hunger, they sought to corrupt the Guardians' Totem and claim its power for themselves. War erupted.

As the battlefield burned and the Totems trembled under the strain of clashing forces… the sky cracked open. A blazing meteor tore through the heavens, crashing into the land. Both sides fell silent, forced to retreat — to recover, regroup, and prepare for the war to continue.

Episode 2 · The rise of warriors

Legends Awaken

When the meteor struck, the sacred Totems cracked. The powers of both the Chaos Horde and the Cronos Guardians waned — fractured, fading.

Yet one Totem stood untouched. Ancient. Unclaimed. It belonged to neither side… and its silence echoed louder than the roar of war.

From the smoldering ruins, the Chaos Horde rose — 250 fierce warriors twisted by fury and forged in chaos.

Across the land, the light answered. The Cronos Guardians emerged — 250 strong, unyielding, forged in duty and hope.

With the mysterious Totem's energy awakening, neither side hesitated. Battle cries rang out. Blades were drawn.

The two armies charged — flame against light, wrath against will.

Episode 3 · A new hope emerges

The Birth of the Trinari

The war raged on. Steel clashed with fury, and light met shadow. Both the Chaos Horde and the Cronos Guardians suffered great losses. Their once-mighty Totems — fractured, dimmed, and fading — no longer fueled the strength they once held.

Yet one Totem remained.

Unclaimed. Undisturbed. And now, pulsing stronger than ever.

The Trinari Totem — born of earth, flame, light, and shadow — awakened.

From the very crater where the meteor had struck, the Trinari emerged.

Mystical, radiant beings — neither Horde nor Guardian. Shaped by the natural world, forged in the impact of celestial fire, and woven with the essence of both rival factions. They carried the glow of balance, the calm of the forest, the fury of storms, and the memory of war.

Though some bore the mark of Chaos… and others carried the light of the Guardians… they were something new.

Something united. The Trinari did not come to conquer. They came to restore.

Peace is their purpose. Healing, their strength. And though the scars of war run deep, the Trinari believe in what others have forgotten:

That peace is still possible.
That even broken things can be made whole.

Now, as Chaos and Guardians prepare for another clash…
The Trinari rise — not to fight, but to stand between.

To offer a new path.

Episode 4 · Balance on the edge

The Turning of the Totems

The age of war had scarred the world.

The Chaos Horde burned with restless fury, and the Cronos Guardians stood weary beneath the weight of endless battle. Both sides had fought for honor, for survival — yet the light of their Totems had dimmed, no longer shining with the same might as before.

Then came the tremor.

The Trinari Totem, the brightest of all, began to pulse — its radiance spilling across the land like a heartbeat from the earth itself. The air shimmered, and the balance of power began to shift.

The Totem of Chaos still burned, but its flame flickered uncertainly.
The Totem of Guardians still glowed, but its calm light quivered with unease.

Something deeper than allegiance was stirring.

Across the lands, Guardians and Chaos warriors alike began to change. Some Guardians felt a pull toward the unknown light of the Trinari, their loyalty shaken by visions of unity. Others within the Chaos ranks found their rage softening, their hearts caught between shadow and peace.

The balance itself trembled — not broken, but unstable. The world felt it in every breath, every whisper of the wind.

Is the power of the Trinari Totem truly strong enough to bring peace?
Or will its light only deepen the cracks between order and chaos?

The Totems still stand. Their power still flows.
Trinari — the brightest of them all.

Episode 5 · The first of the Trinari

The Elderborn

Long before the Trinari awakened in the forests and winds, there was the First Impact.

A celestial meteor streaked across the sky, crashing into the realm with earth-shaking force. It was the same blazing star that silenced the old war. The armies retreated to recover, and no one stayed to watch the crater cool.

So no one saw the very first Trinari emerge: the Elderborn.

Each was born from a single element the meteor's magic had touched. Stone that stood up and carved itself a crown. Lava that cooled into scales but kept its fire. Old wood that sprouted a leaf and a grin. Moss and root that gathered around the first campfire. Shadow that found a pair of red eyes, and a pair of daggers to go with them. And light, golden and warm, that simply refused to put down its hammer.

Earth, flame, light, and shadow — the very forces that would one day wake the Trinari Totem.

Through the long war, the Elderborn kept to the quiet places: the cooling craters, the deep woods, the warm edges of campfires no one remembered lighting.

These are not just Trinari. These are the ancestors. The first. The Elderborn.

Now, as the Trinari Totem shines brightest of all, the ancestors are waking up.
