Defend the forest.

Trinari Defense is a free wave-survival game. You're a Trinari forest guardian, and the Chaos Horde and the Cronos Guardians are closing in from every side. Survive the waves, then leave your name in the Hall of Legends.

How to play

A staff, a dash and a lot of nerve.

Enter a name, press Start game and hold the forest for as long as you can. You start with 10 health, and every wave brings more enemies than the last.

Know your enemies

  • Sword and spear fighters charge straight at you.
  • Archers keep their distance and shoot arrows.
  • Mages hang back and throw magic orbs.
  • Every fifth wave brings a boss: a gold ring means Chaos Horde, a blue ring means Cronos Guardians.

Little secrets

  • Some fallen enemies drop a heart. Grab it to heal.
  • A dash makes you untouchable for a moment.
  • Your bolts change color with every wave.
  • Save the Ultimate for when you're surrounded.
Hall of Legends

Leave your name in the Hall.

When the forest finally falls, your run goes on the Hall of Legends, the game's global leaderboard. It has two boards: most kills and longest survival.

Open it any time with the Leaderboard button in the game.
