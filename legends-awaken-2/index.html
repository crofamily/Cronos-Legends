Factions & divisions

Three factions. Seventeen divisions.

The Trinari arrive 150 strong, in three kinds. The Guardians and the Horde each field 312: seven divisions apiece, plus two specials of their own.

Trinari

150 NFTs · 3 divisions

Mystic spirits born from the primal forces of nature. They may be small, but don't be fooled: their power is immense. The Trinari seek a single purpose: restore balance, or reshape it.

  • Lumivira30 NFTs · the rarest

    The most radiant of all Trinari: ancient beings born of pure essence, guiding light and harmony across the realms.

  • Mistralis50 NFTs

    Balanced Trinari of growing strength and wisdom, embodying the natural bond between life, energy, and creation.

  • Whimkin70 NFTs

    The most common Trinari: curious, lively creatures whose playful nature hides untapped power.

Meet the Elderborn, the very first Trinari

Cronos Guardians

312 NFTs · 7 divisions + 2 Titanlords

The sworn warriors of order across the realms. They rise from ancient traditions, wielding celestial power and unmatched battle discipline, to protect the innocent and uphold honor without compromise.

  • The Ironstride60 NFTs

    Ground warriors: elite defenders of Cronos, forged in unbreakable armor and bound by sacred oaths of valor.

  • Celestial Vanguard60 NFTs

    Radiant champions who descend from the heavens, guardians of cosmic balance and divine light.

  • Arcane Enclave50 NFTs

    Keepers of magic: masters of ancient spells and forbidden knowledge, protectors of Cronos' most powerful secrets.

  • Shadowforged50 NFTs

    The Silent Watch: former Chaos warriors who turned against the darkness, now sworn to guard the light they once tried to destroy.

  • Icelyn Frostguard30 NFTs

    Cold-hearted protectors from the frozen north, whose blades sing with the chill of eternal winter.

  • Inkforged Sigilbearers30 NFTs

    Tattooed mystics of binding oaths, whose ink holds power older than time and seals their bodies with runes of strength and fate.

  • Alloyed Core30 NFTs

    Ancient biomechanical guardians: machines merged with divine essence, keepers of both metal and spirit.

  • Titanlord2 NFTs

    Two special Titanlord pieces stand with the Guardians, apart from the seven divisions.

Chaos Horde

312 NFTs · 7 divisions + 2 ChaosMasters

Savage conquerors driven by raw power, madness, and the pursuit of absolute domination. Born from corrupted magic and forbidden rituals, the Horde grows stronger with every war it sparks.

  • Nightshade Wardens60 NFTs

    Masters of shadow and venom, creeping through darkness to spread the will of Chaos.

  • The Horned Dimension60 NFTs

    Eldritch beings from realms beyond reason, twisting reality itself in their chaotic image.

  • The Shadowborn50 NFTs

    Cursed souls reborn in pure darkness, driven by vengeance and an unending hunger for power.

  • The Deathforged50 NFTs

    Souls bound in armor of bone and flame, warriors who find life only in death's embrace.

  • The Blood Eclipse30 NFTs

    Priests of crimson moons who draw strength from sacrifice, their rituals painting the stars red.

  • The Voidweavers30 NFTs

    Cosmic cultists who command the nothingness between worlds, bending space to their will.

  • Oathbreaker30 NFTs

    Once-noble Guardians who betrayed their vows, now corrupted champions of Chaos and sworn enemies of their former kin.

  • ChaosMaster2 NFTs

    Two special ChaosMaster pieces stand with the Horde, apart from the seven divisions.
