Liquidity

Our liquidity, in the open.

Most $CLG trading happens in one pool: the VVS Finance V3 CLG/CRONUS pool with a 1% fee. Our project wallet supplies most of its liquidity through one full-range position, #49804. Every trade pays the pool 1%, and that fee goes to the liquidity in it.

$CRONUS appears here only because it's the pool's pair token. Every trade in this pool has $CLG on one side and $CRONUS on the other, so the pool and its fees are counted in both.

Getting $CLG

Check twice, buy once.

$CLG trades on VVS Finance, a decentralized exchange on Cronos. Here is how to get the real one.

  1. Start from this pageThe Get $CLG on VVS button opens VVS with the real $CLG already selected.
  2. Check the addressThe only $CLG contract is 0x08d9…3b41. Anyone can create a token called CLG, so match the first and last four characters every time.
  3. Mind the pool and the price impactMost $CLG liquidity sits in the CLG/CRONUS 1% pool; other $CLG pools are tiny and their prices can be far off. With only 500 $CLG in existence, even small trades move the price, so check the price impact before you confirm and keep your slippage tolerance low.
  4. Confirm in your own walletYour wallet shows what you pay and what you receive. No real app or person will ever ask for your recovery phrase.
