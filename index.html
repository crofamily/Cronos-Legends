Burn for $CLG

Retire a legend. Receive $CLG.

Burn a Legends Awaken I, Legends Awaken II or Trinari: Elderborn NFT on crovia.app and the $CLG lands in your wallet in the same transaction, paid by that collection's own claim contract. Burning opens once the Elderborn mint has sold out.

  1. Open the collection on crovia.appConnect your Cronos wallet: Crypto.com Onchain, MetaMask, Rabby and more.
  2. Pick the NFTThe burn panel shows your eligible NFTs and the exact $CLG each one pays.
  3. Approve it, then burnThe NFT goes to 0x…dEaD and $CLG arrives in the same transaction.
Open the burn page Checking…

Legends Awaken burns pay a fixed amount of $CLG, first come, first served while the reserve covers it; each Elderborn burn pays its share of the Elderborn reserve. The amounts are published when burning opens. Read the full rules.

The rules are code

Each collection's claim contract is published and cannot be upgraded. Here is what that means for you:

  • No upgrades, no pause — nobody can change the payout rule or the eligible NFTs
  • Only the reserve wallet may withdraw, and only after a date fixed in the contract — renewed a month at a time, never brought closer
  • No per-wallet limits, no windows, no queues
  • Payouts are counted in $CLG, with no price oracle
  • One approval for the NFT you burn, never a signature

A burn only fails when the reserve can't cover it, and then you keep your NFT. The full rules.
