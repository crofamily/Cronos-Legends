Basics

What is Cronos Legends?

A hand-painted fantasy world on the Cronos blockchain. You can collect its characters as NFTs, read the saga, play Trinari Defense in your browser and hold $CLG, the realm's one token.

Cronos Legends is created and operated by Crovia Oy (Finland), the team behind crovia.app. Every official link and address is listed on our About page.

Who are the three factions?

The Cronos Guardians keep order and light. The Chaos Horde runs on fury and wants the old world gone. And the Trinari, born where a star fell, don't pick sides: they stand between. Their story is on the Lore page.

Why should I trust any of this?

You shouldn't have to take our word for it, so check. $CLG's supply is fixed and its ownership is renounced. The burn contracts have no owner. Our liquidity and its fees are public. The numbers on this site are read live from Cronos, and every address links to the Cronos explorer.

Like any crypto asset, $CLG and the NFTs can lose value. Nothing on this site is financial advice.

$CLG

What is $CLG?

The one token of Cronos Legends. There are exactly 500, all minted once when the token was created in 2024, and its ownership was renounced four days later. It's a plain ERC-20 on Cronos: no mint, no tax, no blacklist and no pause. The details are on our $CLG page.

What is the $CLG contract address?

The only $CLG contract, on Cronos (chain 25), is:

$CLG0x08d9cb5100C306C2909B63415d7ff05268633b41

Anyone can create a token called CLG, so check the first and last four characters every time you paste it.

Where do I get $CLG?

On VVS Finance, a decentralized exchange on Cronos. The Get $CLG on VVS button on our $CLG page opens VVS with the real $CLG already selected. Most $CLG liquidity sits in the VVS V3 CLG/CRONUS 1% pool; other $CLG pools are tiny, and their prices can be far off.

What can I do with $CLG?

It's what the burn program pays: burn a Legends Awaken I or II NFT and you receive $CLG in the same transaction. Holding $CLG also unlocks holder roles in the Discord once you verify your wallet.

Can anyone make more $CLG?

No. The contract has no mint function and its ownership was renounced in 2024, so the supply is 500, forever. And $CLG sent to the burn address 0x…dEaD can never move again, so the amount in circulation can only go down.

What happened to the other community tokens?

Older community tokens were retired. $CLG is the only Cronos Legends token.

Liquidity & fees

Where is $CLG's liquidity?

In the VVS Finance V3 CLG/CRONUS pool with a 1% fee, at 0x3d7e…d818. Our project wallet holds position #49804 in it, which covers the full price range and makes up most of the pool's liquidity. The live numbers are on our $CLG page.

Why does $CRONUS show up?

Only because it's the pool's pair token. Every trade in this pool has $CLG on one side and $CRONUS on the other, so the pool and its fees are counted in both.

What happens to the LP fees?

TBA. Every trade in the pool pays a 1% fee to the liquidity in it, which is mostly our position. What we do with those fees will be published on this site. Until then they stay on-chain, where anyone can see them: the $CLG page shows the fees waiting to collect and the fees collected so far, live.

Who controls the liquidity?

Position #49804 is held by our single-key project wallet, the same wallet that receives NFT royalties. It isn't locked, so the wallet can add or remove liquidity. Any change shows up on the $CLG page as soon as it happens.

Burning

Checking…Open the burn page

How does burning work?

Open the burn page, connect your Cronos wallet, pick one of your Legends Awaken I or II NFTs and confirm once. In that single wallet transaction the NFT goes to the burn address 0x…dEaD and $CLG arrives in your wallet. No token approvals, and never a message to sign.

The burn contracts have no owner and can't be changed. Their addresses are published on this site at launch.

Which NFTs can I burn?

Legends Awaken I (token IDs 1–500) and Legends Awaken II (token IDs 1–774). Trinari: Elderborn can be burned too, but only after its mint has sold out. The Totems of Power live on Cronos POS and aren't part of the burn program.

How much $CLG do I receive?

For each Legends Awaken I or II NFT: $35 of $CLG, and never more than 0.3 CLG. The $CLG price used is the higher of CLG's 24-hour and recent average price on VVS pools.

Trinari: Elderborn pays $15 of $CLG, only after its mint has sold out. The burn page shows the exact amount before you confirm.

Why might I receive less than $35?

Two rules, both on purpose. First, the price used is the higher of CLG's 24-hour and recent average, so right after a sharp fall, the $CLG you receive is worth less than $35 at the current market price. Second, a burn never pays more than 0.3 CLG, so whenever $CLG trades below about $117, 0.3 CLG is worth less than $35. Together they make it pointless to push the price down to squeeze more $CLG out of a reserve.

The burn page also sets a minimum when you confirm. If the price moves past it before your transaction lands, nothing happens and your NFT is still yours.

My NFT is in the Crypto.com NFT app. Can I burn it?

Not from there. Withdraw it to your own Cronos wallet first, for example Crypto.com Onchain, MetaMask or Rabby, then open the burn page. The burn happens in your wallet, so the NFT has to be in it.

What if the reserve is empty?

Then burns wait. Each burn contract pays from its own reserve of $CLG, which the team funds by sending $CLG to it, and it's first come, first served while the reserve lasts. If a reserve can't cover a burn, the transaction doesn't go through: nothing moves and your NFT is still yours. Burns work again once more $CLG arrives, and the burn page shows each reserve live.

What if I send my NFT to 0x…dEaD myself, or use another tool?

You receive nothing, and you can't undo it. Only the Burn button on cronoslegends.com/burn pays, because it sends your NFT straight into the burn contract with safeTransferFrom, and that's what triggers the payout. Sending it to 0x…dEaD yourself, a marketplace's or wallet's burn feature, or a plain transfer to the burn contract pays nothing.

Can I undo a burn?

No. A burned NFT sits at 0x…dEaD forever, and nobody can bring it back, including us. That's why the burn page asks you to tick a box before you confirm. Take a last look at your legend first.

Can the team stop or change the burn program?

Nobody can pause or change the burn contracts: they have no owner, no admin and no upgrade path, and $CLG in a reserve can only leave as a burn payout.

But burns do stop automatically whenever the price data can't be trusted, for example if liquidity is withdrawn from the CLG/CRONUS pool. Most of that liquidity is our own position #49804, so if we removed it, burns would stop while it's out and for up to a day after it returns. Burns also wait when a reserve runs out. Whenever burns are stopped, nothing moves and your NFT stays yours.

Collections & Elderborn

Which collections are there?

Four. Legends Awaken I (500 NFTs) and Legends Awaken II (774 NFTs) launched in 2025 on Crypto.com NFT. Trinari: Elderborn (212) is coming to crovia.app. The Totems of Power are nine artifacts held on Crypto.com NFT. Meet them all on the Collections page.

Where can I view or trade Legends Awaken NFTs?

On crovia.app: Legends Awaken I and Legends Awaken II. Both collections live on Cronos.

What is Trinari: Elderborn?

212 unique Elderborn, the first of the Trinari, each shaped from a single element. Every one is a playable character in Cronos City, and each holder can download its 3D model (Crovia keeps the usage rights in Crovia and Cronos City). The public mint is 400 CRO, with up to 10 per wallet and 5% royalties.

Mint timing lives on the crovia.app launch page, so it's always current. After the mint has sold out, an Elderborn can be burned for $15 of $CLG.

What are the Totems of Power?

Nine artifacts of awakening, three for each faction (Rare, Legendary and Mythic), held on Crypto.com NFT on Cronos POS. They aren't Cronos EVM NFTs and aren't part of the burn program. You can view them on crovia.app.

The Totem of Peace was contributed to Crypto.com's "Art of Giving: STAY with UNICEF" charity drop.

Wallets & safety

Which wallets work?

Any Cronos wallet that connects in your browser, such as Crypto.com Onchain, MetaMask or Rabby. On a phone, open cronoslegends.com/burn in your wallet app's built-in browser. Wallets connect only on the burn page; the rest of the site never asks.

Will you ever ask for my recovery phrase?

Never. We never DM first and never ask for your recovery phrase or private key. Real help happens in public Discord channels or in a support ticket you open yourself.

A burn is one normal transaction that your wallet shows you before you confirm: no approvals, never a message to sign. If anything asks for more, stop.

How do I spot a fake?

Check the address, not the name. Anyone can create a token or an NFT collection called Cronos Legends or CLG. Our official links and contract addresses are all on one page: if it's not listed there, it's not us. Be wary of DMs, "support" accounts that contact you first, airdrops you need to claim, and anything urgent.

What does this site know about me?

Very little. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. If you connect a wallet on the burn page, your address is used in your browser only and never stored by us. The public Cronos RPC services our pages read from, and Google Fonts, can see your IP address. Trinari Defense leaderboard names are stored in Google Firebase. Details are on the Privacy page.

Play & Discord

What can I play?

Trinari Defense, a free wave-survival game in your browser. You're a Trinari forest guardian holding off the Chaos Horde and the Cronos Guardians, on a computer or a phone. No wallet needed.

Is my Trinari Defense score saved?

Yes. When a run ends, your name, kills, wave and survival time go to the Hall of Legends, the game's global leaderboard. Names are stored in Google Firebase and shown publicly, so use a nickname, not your real name.

What happens in the Discord?

It's where holders gather, community games and events run, and news lands first. Verify your wallet there to unlock holder roles for your Legends Awaken NFTs and $CLG. Rules for community games are posted in the Discord.

How do I get help?

Open a support ticket in the Discord, or email contact@cronoslegends.com. We'll never ask for your recovery phrase.

Still wondering?

Ask in the Discord or send us an email. We never DM first, so if someone messages you claiming to be us, it isn't.
