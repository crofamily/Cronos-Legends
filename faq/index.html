Basics

What is Cronos Legends?

A fantasy world on the Cronos blockchain. You can collect its characters as NFTs, read the saga and hold $CLG, the realm's one token. Its Trinari characters will become playable in Cronos City, an upcoming 3D game.

Our collections are available on crovia.app, and every official link and address is listed on our About page.

Who are the three factions?

The Cronos Guardians keep order and light. The Chaos Horde runs on fury and wants the old world gone. And the Trinari, born where a star fell, don't pick sides: they stand between. Their story is on the Lore page.

Why should I trust any of this?

You shouldn't have to take our word for it, so check. $CLG's supply is fixed and its ownership is renounced. $CLG leaves a burn contract only as burn payouts, or to the reserve wallet after a date fixed in the contract that can be pushed later but never brought closer. Our liquidity and its fees are public. The numbers on this site are read live from Cronos, and every address links to the Cronos explorer.

Like any crypto asset, $CLG and the NFTs can lose value. Nothing on this site is financial advice.

$CLG

What is $CLG?

The one token of Cronos Legends. There are exactly 500, all minted once when the token was created in 2024, and its ownership was renounced four days later. It's a plain ERC-20 on Cronos: no mint, no tax, no blacklist and no pause. The details are on our $CLG page.

What is the $CLG contract address?

The only $CLG contract, on Cronos (chain 25), is:

$CLG0x08d9cb5100C306C2909B63415d7ff05268633b41

Anyone can create a token called CLG, so check the first and last four characters every time you paste it.

Where do I get $CLG?

On cro.trade. The Buy $CLG on cro.trade button on our $CLG page opens it on the real $CLG. Most $CLG liquidity sits in our VVS V3 CLG/CRONUS pool, which uses the 1% fee tier, so a swap through it pays 1% to the liquidity. Other $CLG pools are tiny, and their prices can be far off.

What can I do with $CLG?

It's what the burn program pays: burn a Trinari: Elderborn NFT now, or a Legends Awaken I or II NFT once their burning opens, and you receive $CLG in the same transaction. Holding $CLG also unlocks holder roles in the Discord once you verify your wallet.

Can anyone make more $CLG?

No. The contract has no mint function and its ownership was renounced in 2024, so the supply is 500, forever. And $CLG sent to the burn address 0x…dEaD can never move again, so the amount in circulation can only go down.

What happened to the other community tokens?

Older community tokens were retired. $CLG is the only Cronos Legends token.

Liquidity & fees

Where is $CLG's liquidity?

In the VVS Finance V3 CLG/CRONUS pool with a 1% fee, at 0x3d7e…d818. Our project wallet holds position #49804 in it, which covers the full price range and makes up most of the pool's liquidity. The live numbers are on our $CLG page.

Why does $CRONUS show up?

Only because it's the pool's pair token. Every trade in this pool has $CLG on one side and $CRONUS on the other, so the pool and its fees are counted in both.

What happens to the LP fees?

TBA. Every trade in the pool pays a 1% fee to the liquidity in it, which is mostly our position. What we do with those fees will be published on this site. Until then they stay on-chain, where anyone can see them: the $CLG page shows the fees waiting to collect and the fees collected so far, live.

Who controls the liquidity?

Position #49804 is held by our single-key project wallet, the same wallet that receives NFT royalties. It isn't locked, so the wallet can add or remove liquidity. Any change shows up on the $CLG page as soon as it happens.

Burning

Read the burn rules

How does burning work?

You burn on crovia.app, in the burn panel on the collection's page. Connect your Cronos wallet, pick your eligible NFTs, approve them for the collection's claim contract (the one NFT, or the collection once when you burn several), then confirm the burn. In that transaction the NFTs go to the burn address 0x…dEaD and $CLG arrives in your wallet. You never sign a message, and the claim contract can only move NFTs you burn yourself.

Each collection has its own claim contract. Burning is open for Trinari: Elderborn: its claim contract is 0xa3cB…f184, and you burn on crovia.app. For Legends Awaken I and II it isn't open yet: their claim contracts are being set up, their addresses are published on this site when burning opens, and the opening is announced on Discord and X.

Which NFTs can I burn?

Trinari: Elderborn (token IDs 1–212), open now. Legends Awaken I (token IDs 1–500) and Legends Awaken II (token IDs 1–774), once their burning opens. The Totems of Power aren't part of the burn program.

How much $CLG do I receive?

0.1 CLG for each Legends Awaken I, Legends Awaken II or Trinari: Elderborn NFT, the same for every burn, first come, first served while the reserve covers it.

No price oracle is involved: payouts are counted in $CLG, and what they're worth moves with the $CLG market. The burn panel shows the exact amount before you confirm.

My NFT is in the Crypto.com NFT app. Can I burn it?

Not from there. Withdraw it to your own Cronos wallet first, for example Crypto.com Onchain, MetaMask or Rabby, then open the collection on crovia.app. The burn happens in your wallet, so the NFT has to be in it.

What if the reserve is empty?

Then burns wait. Each claim contract pays from its own reserve of $CLG, which is funded by sending $CLG to it, and it's first come, first served while the reserve lasts. If a reserve can't cover a burn, the transaction doesn't go through: nothing moves and your NFT is still yours. Burns work again once more $CLG arrives. The Trinari: Elderborn reserve opened with 2 CLG, enough for the first 20 burns, and more will be added over time.

What if I send my NFT to 0x…dEaD myself, or use another tool?

You receive nothing, and you can't undo it. Only the burn panel on crovia.app pays (or a safeTransferFrom straight into the claim contract), because the claim contract has to receive the NFT itself to pay for it. Sending it to 0x…dEaD yourself, a marketplace's or wallet's burn feature, or a plain transfer to the claim contract pays nothing.

Can I undo a burn?

No. A burned NFT sits at 0x…dEaD forever, and nobody can bring it back, including us. Take a last look at your legend first.

Can the team stop or change the burn program?

Nobody can pause the claim contracts or change their payout rule, eligible NFTs or withdrawal address, and they can't be upgraded.

The one exit: the reserve wallet may withdraw $CLG only after a date fixed in each claim contract. That date can be pushed later but never brought closer, and it is renewed a month at a time; before it, $CLG leaves only as burn payouts. The date only limits withdrawals: burns keep working before, at and after it. Burns also wait when a reserve runs out, and then nothing moves and your NFT stays yours.

Collections & Elderborn

Which collections are there?

Four. Legends Awaken I (500 NFTs) and Legends Awaken II (774 NFTs) launched in 2025 on Crypto.com NFT. Trinari: Elderborn (212 NFTs) was minted out on crovia.app in September 2026. The Totems of Power are nine artifacts held on Crypto.com NFT. Meet them all on the Collections page.

Where can I view or trade the NFTs?

On crovia.app: Legends Awaken I, Legends Awaken II and Trinari: Elderborn. All three collections live on Cronos.

What is Trinari: Elderborn?

212 unique Elderborn, the first of the Trinari, each shaped from a single element. Every one will be playable in Cronos City, the upcoming 3D game at cronos.city, and each holder can download its 3D model. Royalties on secondary sales are 5%.

All 212 were minted on crovia.app in September 2026, so there is no new mint: any "Elderborn mint" link is a scam. To get one now, buy it from a holder on crovia.app. Any Elderborn can also be burned on crovia.app for a fixed 0.1 CLG, first come, first served while the reserve covers it.

What are the Totems of Power?

Nine artifacts of awakening, three for each faction (Rare, Legendary and Mythic), held on Crypto.com NFT, on Cronos. They aren't part of the burn program. You can view them on crovia.app.

The Totem of Peace was contributed to Crypto.com's "Art of Giving: STAY with UNICEF" charity drop.

Wallets & safety

Which wallets work?

Any Cronos wallet that crovia.app supports, such as Crypto.com Onchain, MetaMask or Rabby: burning happens there. On a phone, open crovia.app in your wallet app's built-in browser. This site itself never asks you to connect a wallet.

Will you ever ask for my recovery phrase?

Never. We never DM first and never ask for your recovery phrase or private key. Real help happens in public Discord channels or in a support ticket you open yourself.

A burn happens only on crovia.app, as normal transactions your wallet shows you before you confirm: an approval for the claim contract, then the burn. Never a message to sign, never your recovery phrase, never an approval for any other address. If anything asks for more, stop.

How do I spot a fake?

Check the address, not the name. Anyone can create a token or an NFT collection called Cronos Legends or CLG. Our official links and contract addresses are all on one page: if it's not listed there, it's not us. Be wary of DMs, "support" accounts that contact you first, airdrops you need to claim, and anything urgent.

What does this site know about me?

Very little. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. This site never asks you to connect a wallet. The public Cronos RPC services our pages read from, and Google Fonts, can see your IP address. Details are on the Privacy page.
