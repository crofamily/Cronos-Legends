2. Accessing utilities

To access the utilities provided by the Legends Awaken NFT, holders must join our Discord community. This gives holders access to community events and privileges.

Launch-era utilities

When the collection launched, holders were offered the following utilities. All of them have ended and are no longer offered:

  • EndedA royalty share for holders in Discord during the first two weeks after launch
  • EndedDaily $CLG claims in Discord from a launch reward pool
  • EndedBattleground competitions in Discord with a launch prize pool
  • EndedNFT staking
  • EndedAn NFT buyback fund for marketing
  • EndedDiscord Nitro giveaways for community members
  • EndedA token airdrop for holders
  • EndedA free Legends Awaken II NFT for holders of at least five Legends Awaken NFTs

In-game use
